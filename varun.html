About me

I am an academic leader who has worked across the whole school: as teacher, coordinator, Vice Principal and now Principal. My earlier years in corporate HR and in English training and EdTech shape how I manage people and run schools with discipline.

I like practical solutions: clubs students actually join, tools that save teachers time, and student projects that solve real school problems. Today I focus on bringing AI into the school ecosystem in a practical, future-ready way.

Outside school I am a bird photographer, and I enjoy making things by hand: wall painting, metal art and carpentry.
